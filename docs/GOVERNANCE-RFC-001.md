# GOVERNANCE-RFC-001: Foundational Governance Questions

**Status:** Open

## Purpose

This document records governance questions that must be answered before VerbumScribe is intentionally scaled beyond small trusted communities.

## Emerging Hypothesis

Current product direction suggests that VerbumScribe may be fundamentally a trusted-relationship platform, with public access serving as a structured entry point rather than the primary community model.

This is not yet a ratified governance decision.

Future governance and product decisions should be evaluated against this hypothesis and either confirm it or explicitly reject it.

## Principles Already Accepted

* VerbumScribe exists to support spiritual formation, not maximize engagement.
* Vulnerable disclosures require special stewardship.
* Hosts have meaningful authority within Gatherings.
* Platform operators retain responsibility for addressing abuse, exploitation, manipulation, and harmful conduct.
* Community governance is a first-class concern, not a moderation feature added later.

## Governance Research Goals

The private beta should be treated not only as a product test but also as a governance research exercise.

Observations from real Gatherings should help answer the questions above before constitutional governance documents are written.

## Exit Criteria

This RFC should remain open until sufficient operational experience exists to make informed governance decisions.

When enough evidence has been gathered, decisions may be formalized in a future GOVERNANCE.md document.
