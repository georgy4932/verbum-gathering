# VerbumScribe Product Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Active Development

---

# Product Vision

VerbumScribe is a discipleship platform where believers gather around Scripture, prayer, and meaningful discussion.

The platform is intentionally designed around written engagement rather than passive consumption.

> The Word, with you.

---

# Guiding Principles

1. Build simple first
2. Ship before expanding
3. Prioritize discipleship over features
4. Community before virality
5. Written engagement before media
6. Mobile-first experience

---

# Explicit Non-Goals

The following are intentionally excluded unless strategy changes:

- Social media feed
- Follower counts
- Like systems
- Infinite scrolling
- Livestream-first experience
- Church management software
- Donation platform
- Advertising network

# Product Philosophy

People do not come to VerbumScribe to consume content.

They come to gather around the Word.

Every feature should strengthen:

- Scripture
- Prayer
- Fellowship
- Discipleship

If a feature does not support those outcomes, it should not be built.
