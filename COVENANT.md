Verbum Gathering – Core Identity
Core Statement: A quiet, guided digital space for prayer, Scripture, and shared presence with God
and others.
Four Pillars
1. Presence over activity – The goal is stillness, not interaction volume.
2. Guidance over chaos – Gatherings are led, not driven by noise.
3. Formation over engagement – Spiritual growth over metrics.
4. Truth over performance – No artificial signals or false presence.
Three Rules
1. Does this make the space quieter or louder?
2. Does this remove pressure or add pressure?
3. Does this point people inward/upward or outward?
What This App Is Not
Not a social network. Not a chat app. Not performance-driven.
What This App Is
A digital gathering space with stillness, rhythm, and shared focus.
Language Principles
Use: Enter, Remain, Share quietly, Be still.
Avoid: Join now, Engage, Start chatting.
Guiding Principle
Every feature must help someone draw nearer to God, not distract them.
